Digital Safety Resources and CISA Presentations for Your School

K-12 | Free | Administrators, educators, counselors, and families

Thank you to everyone who attended Digital Safety in the Age of AI. The session resources are at education.alaska.gov/cybersecurity, organized by role for administrators, educators, counselors, families, and students.

Spotlight on Alaska

iDidaContest 2027: What Are Your Students Making?

Grades K-12 and ASTE members | Class or individual project | Cash prizes

Much of the national conversation about technology in schools is about how to limit it. ASTE's iDidaContest starts from the other end: students using technology to create something worth sharing. It is Alaska's longest-running digital media contest for students and educators.

The 2027 contest has nine categories covering video, sound, design, publishing, games and interactive media, and AI-enhanced work. iDidaRoots is for projects that honor, explore, or revitalize Indigenous languages, traditions, and knowledge systems. Each category lists the Alaska standards it connects to, and students compete in elementary, middle, and high school divisions. ASTE members can enter their own work.

Teachers can build a class project around one category, and schools can run a local contest and send in their top entries. Families can ask their school about entering. Entries are due January 31, 2027.

STEM for Change in Southeast Alaska

Students | Five-day program | Juneau and Klawock

This fall, Sealaska Heritage Institute is partnering with McKenzie's Adventures to run STEM for Change, a five-day technology program for Alaska Native youth, with cohorts in Juneau and Klawock. Students chose a challenge in their own community, such as language revitalization, fisheries, sustainable tourism, or small business. Students research their, build a working prototype, and pitch their solutions to Elders, educators, and local industry representatives.

Students also prepare for and sit for the Microsoft Azure Fundamentals certification exam, an industry-recognized credential in cloud computing.

The top three teams from each cohort will present their work at ASTE 2027 in February as part of iDidaImpact, a new iDidaContest category. Watch the iDidaContest page for details.

Learn about STEM for Change | Follow STEM for Change on Instagram | See the iDidaContest page

Curriculum Corner

Day of AI's New PreK-5 Curriculum

Grades PreK-5 | Unplugged lessons | Free | Educators

Day of AI released a free, grade-by-grade AI literacy curriculum for PreK through grade 12 in August. The early grades are built so young students learn how AI works without using AI tools. Pre-K starts with people, nature, and machines. Grade 1 looks at how technology finds patterns and uses them to make predictions. Grade 2 covers how data is collected and how AI learns from examples.

Lessons come with slides, student activities, and assessments. Day of AI ran free webinars on the PreK-5 units in late September; check its professional development page for recordings and upcoming sessions.

For Leaders

Student AI Use Is Rising Faster Than School Policy

Administrators | 10-minute read | Free

RAND's American Youth Panel found that AI use for homework among students in middle school and up rose from 48% in May 2025 to 62% in December 2025. In December, 67% agreed that more AI use for schoolwork will harm critical thinking. About a third said their school had a schoolwide AI policy.

Source: RAND, published March 17, 2026. Survey collected December 2025, 1,214 respondents ages 12-29.

Read the RAND report
